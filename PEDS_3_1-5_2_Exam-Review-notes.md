# PEDS Exam Review — Toddlers → Communicable Diseases (3.1–5.2)

## Cram sheet

**Development by age**

| Age | Erikson · Piaget | Play | Biggest hospital stressor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Toddler 1–3** | Autonomy vs. shame & doubt | Solitary → parallel → peer (**2–3**) | ==Separation anxiety== |
| **Preschool 3–6** | Initiative vs. guilt · preoperational (**2–7**) | Associative, pretend | Bodily injury; illness = punishment |
| **School-age 6–12** | Industry vs. inferiority · concrete operations `[ADDED — not in course sources]` | Rules, teams, competition | Loss of routine, friends, school |
| **Adolescent 12–20** | Identity vs. role confusion · formal operations `[ADDED]` | Peers (digital), sports | Body image, ==privacy==, peers |

**Numbers by age**

| Item | Toddler | Preschool | School-age · Teen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Growth/yr | **4–6 lb**, **2–3 in** | ~**4.5 lb**, ~**3 in** | **4–7 lb**, **2–2.5 in** · teen spurt **4–6 in** |
| Sleep | **11–14 hr**/24 hr | ~**12 hr** (**11–13**) · 5.1: **10–13** | **9–12 hr** · teen **8–10** |
| Activity | **≥ 180 min** play | **≥ 180 min**, **60** vigorous | **≥ 60 min**/day; muscle/bone **≥ 3 d/wk** |
| kcal/kg/day `[5.1]` | **80** (1–3 yr) | **70** (4–5 yr) | **65** (6–8) · **35–45** (9+) |

**Toddler vitals:** HR **80–140** (**> 140** → report) · RR **25–30** · SpO2 **95–100%** · routine BP from **age 3**, cuff ≈ **80%** of limb

**Other numbers:** fever **≥ 100.4 °F**; antipyretic if uncomfortable or **> 102.0 °F** · cow's milk **≤ 16–24 oz** (more → IDA) · juice **≤ 4 oz** · time-out **1 min/yr of age** · tantrums **> 5/day** or **> 15 min** → report · regression **> 2–3 wk** → report · screens: developmental **9, 18, 30 mo**, ASD **18 & 24 mo** · sentences: **2** words at **24 mo**, **3** at **36 mo**, **> 4** by **age 4** · water heater **≤ 120 °F** · bus **≥ 10 ft** from curb · teen HTN **≥ 130/80** · sodium (teen) **< 2,300 mg** · diaper **1 g = 1 mL** · **1 kg = 2.2 lb**

**Car seat:** rear-facing **≥ 2 yr** *and* to seat limit → 5-point harness → booster until belt fits (~**4 ft 9 in**, **9–12 yr**) → back seat until **13**

**Drugs:** **Acetaminophen** — fever/pain; max **5 days**; hepatotoxic · ==**never aspirin** in viral illness (Reye)== · **Ibuprofen** — NSAID; ==not if dehydrated== · **Imipramine** — TCA for enuresis; cardiac eval first · **Desmopressin** — ADH; ==hyponatremia== · **Lisinopril** — ACE-I; cough, ↑ K+, angioedema; teratogenic · **Fluoxetine** — SSRI; ==suicidality==, serotonin syndrome · **Levonorgestrel** — EC within **72 hr**, not regular BC · **Mupirocin** — impetigo · **Permethrin** — scabies/lice (repeat **1 wk**) · **Mebendazole** — pinworm, whole household (repeat **2 wk**) · **Metronidazole** — giardia, BV; no alcohol · **Vitamin A** × **2 days** — measles · **Acyclovir** — HSV stomatitis, viral meningitis

**Report now:** inspiratory stridor · any suspected FBA · bruising off bony prominences, bite marks, immersion burns, spiral fractures · caregiver hitting → state authorities · bullying with weapon/sexual/cyber/hate → law enforcement · ==suicidal ideation== · testicular pain (torsion) · fever + ==petechial rash== · sudden LUQ/left-shoulder pain in mono · IV site cool/swollen or red/warm · restrained limb cool/pale

**Priority:** ABCs (**< 1 min**) → Maslow physiologic → safety · stridor > tachypnea · cyanosis = late · abuse evidence > appearance · ==safety threat to self/others first== (teen) · ==airborne room before anything else== (measles, varicella)

**Isolation:** **Airborne** (negative pressure, N95) = measles, varicella (+ contact), TB · **Droplet** (mask **< 3 ft**) = diphtheria (+ contact), mumps, meningococcal, pertussis, rubella · **Contact** (gown + gloves on entry) = polio, varicella, diphtheria, scabies, lice, impetigo
**PPE off:** ==gloves → goggles → gown → mask== → hand hygiene

**Contrasts:** roseola = high fever **then** rash · fifth = slapped cheek, not contagious once rash shows · measles = Koplik spots, hairline down · primary enuresis (never dry) vs. secondary (dry **≥ 6 mo**) · fear vs. phobia (disrupts daily life) · infiltration (cool) vs. phlebitis (warm) · bacterial (purulent, crusted) vs. viral (watery) vs. allergic (itchy, both eyes) conjunctivitis

**Mnemonics:** **BEARS** (sleep) — Bedtime issues, Excessive daytime sleepiness, Awakenings, Regularity, Snoring · **AIDET** — Acknowledge, Introduce, Duration, Explanation, Thank you · ==Hepatitis: "vowel from the bowel"== — **A**, **E** fecal-oral

## I. Across the ages: concepts the exam reuses

> [!quote] Anchor
> Every age question is really "what can this child understand, and what do they fear?" Match the explanation, the play, and the safety teaching to the stage.

#### Erikson and Piaget: how does the nurse apply them?
| Age | Stage | Nurse application |
|---|---|---|
| **Toddler** | Autonomy vs. shame & doubt; egocentric | ==Offer choices that can't be answered "no"==: "Red shirt or orange shirt?" |
| **Preschool** | Initiative vs. guilt; preoperational: magical thinking, animism, egocentrism | Let the child hold the med cup or oral syringe; ==answer questions seriously==; correct self-blame |
| **School-age** | Industry vs. inferiority · concrete operations `[ADDED — not in course sources]` | Keep routine tasks and schoolwork; age-appropriate explanation of why |
| **Adolescent** | Identity vs. role confusion `[ADDED]`; abstract thought, metacognition `[Engage]` | ==Involve in plan of care==; privacy; adolescent is the main historian |

#### Response to hospitalization by age? `[5.1]`
| Age | What you see | Nurse approach |
|---|---|---|
| **Infant** | Distress; can't understand why | ==Consistent caregivers==, familiar objects, caregiver present |
| **Toddler** | Clings, cries, ==regresses==; separation anxiety peaks | Consistent caregivers and routine, play, choices |
| **Preschool** | ==Pain/illness = punishment==; fears lasting harm | Reassure it's not their fault; pretend play; let them ask |
| **School-age** | Understands why; sad, may withdraw or act out | Routine activities, age-appropriate info, talk about feelings, peer contact |
| **Adolescent** | Acts out or withdraws; body image, ==privacy==, peers | Involve in care, offer choices, respect privacy, keep peer contact digitally |

#### Separation anxiety: three phases? `[5.1, ADDED]`
- **Protest** (screams, cries for parent, rejects strangers) → **despair** (withdrawn, ↓ play and food) → **detachment** (ignores parent on return).
- ==Detachment is not adjustment.== Maximize parental presence. If parents must leave: tell the child and staff when they'll be back; nurse updates by phone.

#### Preparing a child for a procedure?
| Age | Approach |
|---|---|
| **Toddler** | Comfort hold on parent's lap; let them handle safe equipment; simple phrases, puppet demo |
| **Preschool** | ==Demonstrate on a doll first==; explain what they'll **see, hear, feel**; let them open the swab; ==don't show the needle==; parent present |
| **School-age** | Explain germs/body in concrete terms; role-play; let them help |
| **Adolescent** | Full explanation, personal responsibility, privacy |
| **All ages** | Painful procedures ==in the treatment room, never the bed== `[5.1]`; honest: "Yes, it will hurt, but only for a few seconds" `[3.2]` |

- Preschool words: "**poke**," not "stick" or "bee sting" · "**medicine**," not "dye" · "**a different kind of sleep**," not "put to sleep." Never "it won't hurt."

#### Leading injury risks by age?
| Age | Top risk | Key teaching |
|---|---|---|
| **Toddler** | ==Drowning== (leading injury death **1–4 yr**, peak **12–36 mo**); falls; burns; poisoning; FBA (**~80%** of FBAs **< 3 yr**) | Layered water protection; gates top **and** bottom of stairs, no walkers; meds locked; quarter grapes; ==never a blind finger sweep== |
| **Preschool** | Unintentional injury (**75.8%** at home if **≤ 4 yr**); drowning **< 4**, ==MVC > 4== | Booster seat; helmet every ride; trampoline discouraged **< 6** |
| **School-age** | ==Bicycle== (highest risk); bus; firearms | Helmet; ==never walk behind the bus==; guns unloaded, locked, ammo separate |
| **Adolescent** | ==MVC== | Seat belt; ==no phone while driving==; sports gear |

> [!info] Key contrast
> "Leading cause of injury death" depends on the age band in the question: **drowning 1–4**, **MVC > 4** (preschool) and adolescence, **firearms** for all **< 18** combined `[4.1 Engage]`.

#### Car seat fit rules?
- **Middle of the back seat.** Chest clip at ==armpit level==; no pinchable slack; no bulky coat under the harness.
- Harness at or **below** the shoulders rear-facing; at or **above** forward-facing.
- LATCH **or** seat belt, not both; replace after **any** crash; seats expire ~**6 yr**.

#### Regression: expected or report?
- Expected under stress: new baby, hospitalization, illness, move. Baby talk, bottle, bedwetting, thumb sucking.
- In hospital: training pants; ==reassure parents it's temporary==; never punish `[5.1]`.
- Report if **> 2–3 weeks** or with other manifestations `[3.1]`. In school-age, new enuresis or regression can signal ==stress== `[4.1]`.

#### Discipline teaching across ages?
- Time-out ==**1 min per year of age**== (2-yr-old ≤ **2 min**; 7-yr-old ~**7 min**).
- Tantrums: normal, unavoidable; ==adult stays calm==. Report extreme aggression, **> 5/day**, **> 15 min**.
- ==Corporal punishment worsens behavior==: ↑ aggression, ↓ self-esteem, depression, substance use, self-harm, suicide attempts. Use positive reinforcement, consistency, logical consequences.

#### Mandated reporting: which triggers?
| Situation | Report to |
|---|---|
| Abuse/neglect red flags; caregiver hitting, slapping, hair-pulling | ==State social services/CPS== |
| Bullying with weapon, serious harm, sexual/cyber, or hate threats | **Law enforcement** |
| Sexual assault of an adolescent | Mandated report (all nurses) |
| Some positive STIs; nationally notifiable diseases (measles, varicella, pertussis, hepatitis, COVID-19…) | **Health department** / NNDSS |

- Reporting ==starts an investigation; it doesn't automatically remove the child==.

#### Dental care by age?
- First dental visit at first tooth or by **12 mo**, then every **6 months**. Parent brushes until the child is proficient; ==supervise brushing until **10**==.
- **Fluoride** toothpaste **2×/day** from first tooth `[4.1]` (3.1 deck said "fluoride-free"; see Source notes). Sealants; juice **≤ 4 oz**; no bottles or sippy cups pooling on teeth.
- Caries = ==most common chronic childhood disease==.

#### Prioritization: who first?
| Situation | First |
|---|---|
| Respiratory distress | ==ABCs== in **< 1 min**; inspiratory stridor beats RR **45**, crackles, dry cough |
| Unkempt child + bruises on abdomen/buttocks + won't bear weight | Abuse evidence → CPS, x-ray (Raya: spiral femur fx) |
| Child lacks food + delays | Physiologic need (food access) first (Marcus) |
| Teen with SI + poor A1C + delayed puberty | ==Suicide risk== → notify provider |
| Measles/varicella admission | Airborne room before anything else |
| Mono + sudden LUQ/left shoulder pain | Splenic rupture → shock |
| Fever + petechial rash | Meningococcemia → emergency |
| Diphtheria + stridor/drooling | Airway obstruction |

## II. Toddlers, 1–3 yr (PEDS 3.1)

> [!quote] Anchor
> Growth slows; cognition, language, and autonomy accelerate. Autonomy drives most "misbehavior."

### Assessment

#### Which temperature route, and when not rectal?
| Route | Key point |
|---|---|
| **Axillary** | Most common, safe; ==not a core temp== |
| **Tympanic** | Pull pinna ==down and back== (**< 3 yr**) |
| **Temporal** | Swipe forehead |
| **Rectal** | ==Most dangerous==; contraindicated with diarrhea, coagulation disorders, immunocompromise, rectal surgery |

- High/low axillary → recheck ==tympanic or temporal== for core.

#### When to treat a fever?
- Fever **≥ 100.4 °F (38.0 °C)**. First rest + ↑ oral fluids. Antipyretic if uncomfortable, no response, or **> 102.0 °F (38.8 °C)**. ==Get a current weight first== (weight-based dosing).
- Cooling: tepid water **85–90 °F**; ==never cold== (shivering ↑ temp).

#### Acetaminophen vs. ibuprofen?
| Drug | Adverse effects | Teaching / contraindications |
|---|---|---|
| **Acetaminophen** | ==Hepatotoxicity==; SJS/TEN, rash | Max **5 days**; stop if rash; dose by weight with supplied device; not with liver disease |
| **Ibuprofen** (NSAID) | GI upset, bleeding, renal failure, ↑ K+ | Max **3 days** fever / **10 days** pain; ==not if dehydrated== (vomiting, diarrhea, poor intake); not with bleeding disorders, kidney disease, HF |

#### Respiratory distress: what's priority?
- Signs: ↑ work of breathing, tachypnea, retractions, stridor, nasal flaring, tripod/sniffing position. ==Cyanosis is late.==

> [!danger] Report immediately
> **Inspiratory stridor** = upper airway obstruction → priority over tachypnea, diminished bases, or cough. ABC check takes **< 1 min**; history and full exam after the child is stable.

### Development

#### Toddler milestones by age?
| Age | Motor | Language / social |
|---|---|---|
| **15 mo** | Few independent steps; stacks **2** objects | |
| **18 mo** | Walks alone; scribbles; tries utensils | **5+** words (deck) / **3+** (module) |
| **24 mo** | Runs, kicks ball, stairs ± help | ==**2-word** sentences==; names **≥ 2** body parts |
| **30 mo** | Jumps with both feet; turns **1** page | ~**50** words |
| **36 mo** | ==Rides tricycle==; dresses partly | ==**3-word** sentences==; plays with other children |

- Missed milestone → ==document and notify the provider==. Risks: prematurity, low birth weight, lead.
- **Corrected age**: subtract weeks born early (born **6 wk** early → subtract **6 wk**); usually not needed after **age 2**.

#### ASD: signs and screening tool?
- Evident by ~**18 mo**: poor eye contact, no response to name, ==lining up objects==, repetitive behavior, ==speech regression==. ==Vaccines don't cause ASD.==
- Screening **doesn't diagnose**; positive → full provider evaluation + early intervention.

| Tool | Age |
|---|---|
| **ITC** | **6–24 mo** |
| **M-CHAT-R/F** | **16–30 mo** |
| **POSI** | **16–35 mo** |
| **STAT** | **24–36 mo**, already flagged |

### Behavior

#### Toilet-training readiness?
- Ready **2–4 yr**; don't start **< 18 mo–2 yr**. Spinal cord myelinated by **2** → senses fullness.
- Signs: pulls pants up/down, ==communicates the urge==, dry several hours or after nap.
- ==Resistance = not ready== → stop, retry in **2–3 months**. Praise attempts; never punish accidents; potty chair with feet on the floor; avoid starting during stress (new baby).

#### Sibling rivalry: what helps?
- ==Never leave the toddler alone with the baby.== Big changes (new room, toilet training, weaning) months **before or after** the baby. One-on-one time; don't compare.

### Health promotion and concerns

#### Toddler nutrition: what's tested?
- **1,000–1,400 kcal/day** in **4–6** small meals; picky eating expected → keep re-offering.
- ==Whole milk **< 2 yr**==; milk **≤ 16–24 oz/day**; water over juice (**≤ 4 oz**).
- Choking: ==quarter grapes==, smash blueberries, hot dogs in strips quartered lengthwise; avoid popcorn, nuts, hard candy, raw carrots. Child seated while eating.

#### Iron deficiency anemia: why toddlers?
- ==**> 16–24 oz** cow's milk== (low iron, displaces food) or too few iron-rich foods. Lead poisoning ↑ risk.
- Screen ~**12 mo** (any time **9–24 mo**). Prevent: **≥ 3** iron-rich servings or **≥ 7 mg/day**.

#### Lead: who's at risk?
- ==No safe level.== Pre-**1978** paint, soil, water, imported goods, parental jobs.
- Toddler risk: hand-to-mouth, incomplete blood-brain barrier, ==IDA ↑ lead absorption==.

#### Food insecurity screen?
- **Hunger Vital Sign** (past **12 months**): worried food would run out · food didn't last. ==Either "often" or "sometimes true" = positive== → SNAP, WIC, TANF, food banks.

#### Poisoning and FBA response?
- ==Never call medicine "candy."== Lock meds (high shelf ≠ locked).
- Ingestion: remove product, child spits out, ==call poison control; don't induce vomiting== unless told.

> [!danger] Report immediately
> Any suspected **FBA** (e.g., pointing at nose, blowing hard after eating, nothing visible) → ==assessed by a provider immediately==. Don't reassure over the phone.

#### Sleep and sun?
- **11–14 hr/24 hr**; cool, dark room; electronics off **≥ 1 hr** before bed (none **< 2 yr**). Screen with **BEARS**.
- Sunscreen SPF **≥ 15**, **30 min** before, reapply ~**q2h**; avoid sun **10 a.m.–4 p.m.**

## III. Preschoolers, 3–6 yr (PEDS 3.2)

> [!quote] Anchor
> Refinement, not rapid growth: smoother motor skills, an explosion of language, initiative, and social play.

### Development

#### Motor milestones?
- Gross: ==hops and skips on one foot==, alternates feet on stairs, **overhand** throw. Needing support to balance = earlier stage.
- Fine: pincer grasp; shapes circle → square → triangle → diamond; drawing a person with **3** parts at **4 yr**, **6** at **5 yr**; cuts shapes.
- Growth ~**3 in**/yr: **40 in** at 4 → expect ~**43 in** at 5. ==No height gain in a year → assess.==

#### Preoperational concepts: which is which?
| Concept | Example |
|---|---|
| **Transductive reasoning** | Dog barks, balloon pops → "the dog popped it" |
| **Centration** | Counts cake pieces, ignores size |
| **Egocentrism** | Comforts a sad adult with *their own* stuffed animal |
| **Magical thinking / animism** | =="I got sick because I was mean to my sister"==; toys are alive |
| **Assimilation → accommodation** | Calls a cow a dog → learns "cow," builds a new schema |

> [!tip] NCLEX tip
> Use magical thinking, don't fight it: ==assess the doll first==, let the child "help," and ==ask the child what made them sick== to correct self-blame.

#### Language: expected vs. delay?
- **> 2,100** words; sentences **> 4** words by **4**. ==At 4, "I hungry" = delay.== Literal thinkers (see word swaps in I).
- Speech delay → ==prompt speech pathologist referral==. New stutter often follows situational change (school, hospitalization, home).

### Behavior

#### Aggression: expected or concerning?
- Peaks through **3**, should ==↓ significantly by **5**==. Types: physical, verbal, **relational** (exclusion, "silent treatment").
- Concerning: repetitive patterns, consistent aggressor/victim roles, ==intent to harm==. Harsh discipline ↑ aggression; warm parenting ↓ it.

#### Masturbation: nurse response?
- ==Common **2–5 yr**==, self-exploration. Don't punish; ==redirect==, teach privacy. Can signal UTI or stress.

### Safety and prevention

#### Preschool vaccines and site?
- **4–6 yr**: ==**DTaP, IPV, MMRV**== + yearly flu. (Not hep B.)
- ==Deltoid preferred **3–10 yr**==; thigh also OK. ==Avoid dorsogluteal== (sciatic nerve).

#### Trampoline and bike teaching?
- Trampoline ==discouraged **< 6 yr**==; AAP discourages home use. If used: net, pads, one jumper, no flips.
- Tricycle at **3**; ==helmet every ride== (↓ head injury **48%**); no street riding; away from driveways.

### Abuse and neglect

#### Physical abuse red flags?
| Visible | Radiologic |
|---|---|
| ==Bruises off bony prominences==: chest, abdomen, back, buttocks, GU, ears, neck | **Spiral**, multiple **rib**, **scapular**, complex skull fractures |
| ==Any bite mark== | Fractures in different stages of healing |
| **Immersion** burns (symmetric, sharp line); patterned contact burns | Bilateral subdural + retinal hemorrhage (abusive head trauma) |

- Skin findings in **90%** of physical abuse. Full-body exam; document ==direct quotes==; story doesn't match → report.

> [!danger] Report immediately
> Report to **state social services**. Script: *"We are concerned for the safety of your child. Based on what was found today, we are required to report to state services for further investigation."*

#### FDIA: nurse's action?
- Caregiver fabricates or ==induces== illness (formerly Munchausen by proxy); doctor-shopping. Nurse: ==thoroughly document== reports and inconsistencies; team; stay unbiased.

#### Sexual abuse prevention teaching?
- ==**90%** by someone known and trusted.== Teach correct anatomical names, private parts = under the swimsuit, may refuse hugs, ==surprises OK, secret touching never==.
- Child discloses a secret → ==never promise to keep it==.

#### Neglect approach?
- Signs: malnutrition, poor hygiene, fear of parents, withdrawal. Approach with concern, not accusation: "Is there anything we can do to make things easier at home?"

## IV. School-age, 6–12 yr (PEDS 4.1)

> [!quote] Anchor
> School becomes the child's world: peer acceptance and accomplishment drive development.

### Development

#### Key school-age milestones?
- Teeth: permanent teeth from ~**6**. Puberty ~**10** F, ~**12** M `[Engage]`; menarche **2–3 yr** after breast budding.
- Ties shoes **6**; dresses/grooms self **9**. Attention **15 min** at 6 → **1 hr** by 9. Follows ==**5** commands in a row by **10**==.
- ==Peer acceptance is crucial==; same-sex friend groups.
- Missed milestone (e.g., **6-yr-old** can't hold a pencil) → ==early intervention referral==.

### Behaviors

#### Bullying: when to suspect, when to report?
- Peaks in ==middle school==. Suspect with ==poor school attendance==, sleep problems, poor appetite, psychosomatic complaints, depression, ↓ grades. Interview the child ==privately==.

> [!danger] Report immediately
> ==Law enforcement== if bullying involves a **weapon**, **serious bodily harm**, **sexual** or **cyber** bullying, or **hate-motivated** threats.

#### Gender identity: best question?
- =="How do you feel on the inside?"== For orientation, words like "crush." Concealing identity → worse health outcomes; reassess periodically.

#### Fear vs. phobia?
| | Fear | Phobia |
|---|---|---|
| Course | Mild, transient, resolves with reassurance | Persistent, irrational, ==disrupts daily life== |
| Action | Reassure | Refer to **behavioral therapist** |

- 11-yr-old sleeping on parents' floor **3 months** from fear of the dark → phobia → refer.

### Safety

#### Bus and firearm teaching?
- Bus: wait **≥ 10 ft** from curb; board on driver's signal; ==never walk behind the bus==; eye contact before crossing in front.
- Firearms: ==safest = no gun in the home==; else unloaded, locked, ==ammo stored separately==, no child access to keys. ==Ask about storage== before visits (**> 1/3** of unintentional child shootings occur in others' homes).
- Wrong answer: "make sure the child knows where the gun is."

### Health concerns

#### LD and ADHD: what's tested?
- **LD**: ==average or above IQ==; not fixed by glasses/hearing aids. **Dyslexia** (reading) most common (**≥ 80%**). 8-yr-old who can't print name → assess. IEP for school support.
- **ADHD**: onset ==before **12**==; symptoms persist, impair function, ==in **≥ 2** settings==. Treatment manages, doesn't cure. Stimulants: ↓ appetite, insomnia → morning dose, track height/weight `[ADDED]`.

> [!tip] NCLEX tip
> Parent blames self for ADHD: ==validate and correct==: "The cause is unknown, and many parents feel this way."

#### Pediatric BMI categories?
| Class | BMI percentile |
|---|---|
| Overweight | **85th–< 95th** |
| Obese | ==**≥ 95th**== |

- Use ==neutral words ("weight," "BMI")==, not "fat" or "obese." Non-food rewards; family activity.

#### Enuresis: primary vs. secondary?
| | Primary | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | ==Never== dry at night | Dry **≥ 6 mo**, then wets again |
| Cause | Developmental delay | ==UTI==, neuro problem, **stress** |
| Action | Usually outgrown | ==Provider evaluation== |

#### Enuresis meds?
| Drug | Watch for | Teaching |
|---|---|---|
| **Imipramine** (TCA) | Anticholinergic effects, ==arrhythmia==; suicidality boxed warning `[ADDED — verify]` | ==Cardiac evaluation first==; PO **60 min** before bed; ==hold **2 wk** every **3 mo**== |
| **Desmopressin** (ADH) | ==Hyponatremia== (headache, N/V, confusion, seizure) | PO **60 min** before bed; restrict fluids; taper to stop; not with renal impairment |

#### Encopresis: what's really happening?
- Usually ==chronic constipation==: liquid stool leaks around impaction → "diarrhea" on underwear in a toilet-trained child.
- Teach: ==high fiber, plenty of water, regular toileting routine==; positive reinforcement, no shaming.

#### PTSD: when is it concerning?
- Symptoms ==**> 1 month**== interfering with life: nightmares, avoidance, numbing, withdrawal. ==Can mimic ADHD.==
- After a parent's death: crying relieved by comfort is normal; **> 1 month** withdrawal and refusing activities is not.

## V. Adolescents, 12–20 yr (PEDS 4.2)

> [!quote] Anchor
> The bridge from dependence to independence: a growth spurt and sexual maturation outside, abstract thought and identity inside.

### Development

#### Puberty: timing and first signs?
| | Females | Males |
|---|---|---|
| Onset `[Engage]` | **8–13** | **9–14** |
| First sign | ==Breast budding== | ==Testicular enlargement== |
| Growth stops | **2–2.5 yr** after menarche (menarche **12–14**) | **18–20** |

- Growth spurt **4–6 in/yr** → ==temporary clumsiness==. Delayed puberty (often poor nutrition or chronic illness) → ==treat the underlying condition first==, endocrinology referral.

#### Interview approach?
- ==The adolescent is the main source of information.== Interview privately for part of the visit; explain confidentiality limits (harm to self/others, abuse) `[ADDED]`.
- Open-ended questions; ==let them choose their own labels== for orientation; don't avoid trauma questions.
- Some risk-taking is ==expected==; reckless risk-taking is the concern.

> [!tip] NCLEX tip
> Withdrawn teen with negative self-talk: ==first assess== self-esteem with open-ended questions. Peer pressure: ==open communication and active listening==, not strict rules.

### Health promotion

#### Key adolescent numbers and nutrients?
- Sleep ==**8–10 hr**== (puberty shifts sleep later); activity ~**1 hr/day**; ==calcium== for bone (↓ osteoporosis later).
- Sleep teaching: limit devices before bed, relaxation, no caffeine. ==Not benzodiazepines or bedtime TV.==

#### Eating disorder red flags?
- ==Food hoarding, secrecy==, eating alone, rapid weight change, ==lanugo==, feeling cold, thinning hair, fatigue, fainting.
- Anorexia: bradycardia, hypotension, hypothermia, amenorrhea `[ADDED]`. Bulimia: binge + purge, ==hypokalemia==, dental erosion `[ADDED]`. Binge eating: no purging.

### Reproductive and physical concerns

#### Hypertension and lisinopril?
- Adolescent HTN ==**≥ 130/80**==. Lifestyle first (sodium **< 2,300 mg**, weight, activity, no smoking/alcohol), then ACE-I, ARB, CCB.

| Lisinopril (ACE-I) | |
|---|---|
| Adverse effects | ==Dry cough==, ==hyperkalemia==, hypotension, dizziness |
| Contraindications | ==Pregnancy==, angioedema history, severe ↑ K+ |
| Teaching | ==No salt substitutes== (K+), no NSAIDs, no alcohol; report cough |

> [!danger] Report immediately
> Face, lip, tongue, or throat swelling on an ACE-I = ==angioedema== (airway) `[ADDED]`. Sudden severe testicular pain, swelling, discoloration = ==testicular torsion==, a surgical emergency (peak **12–18 yr**).

#### PMS vs. PMDD?
| | PMS | PMDD |
|---|---|---|
| Findings | Bloating, breast tenderness | ==Severe mood==: depression, irritability, anxiety |
| Course | Luteal phase; ends with menses | Same; ==may need SSRI== |

#### Vaginal infections and STIs?
| Condition | Key finding | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **BV** | ==Fishy== thin gray discharge `[ADDED]` | **Metronidazole** (no alcohol) or clindamycin × **7 days** |
| **Chlamydia / gonorrhea** | ==Discharge + dysuria==; often asymptomatic | Antibiotics; treat partners `[ADDED]` |
| **Herpes** | ==Painful== sores | ==Not curable==; antivirals |
| **Syphilis** | ==Painless chancre== | Treatable |
| **HPV** | Warts; cervical/anal cancer | ==Vaccine== |

- Don't assume school covered STIs. Contraception: ==assess knowledge, then tailor==. Some positive STIs ==must be reported to the health department==; STI testing need not be disclosed to guardians.

#### Levonorgestrel (Plan B) teaching?
- ==Within **72 hr**== (moderate effect to **5 days**); ==not regular birth control==; no STI protection; ==does not end an existing pregnancy==.
- Barrier/abstinence **7 days** after; period late **> 7 days** → provider.

#### Teen pregnancy and assault?
- Pregnancy risks: ==preterm birth, low birth weight, high BP==.
- Adolescents have the ==highest sexual assault risk== of any age; ==all nurses are mandated reporters==.

### Mental health

#### Fluoxetine teaching?
| | |
|---|---|
| Watch for | ==Suicidal ideation== (boxed warning `[ADDED]`), ==serotonin syndrome== (agitation, tachycardia, hyperthermia, sweating, rigidity), insomnia, QT prolongation |
| Avoid | MAOIs, other serotonergic drugs, ==St. John's wort==, NSAIDs/warfarin (bleeding) |
| Teaching | Take ==in the morning==; ==don't stop abruptly==; report SI, palpitations, sweating, mood change |

- Anxiety: CBT first; SSRIs ==only with therapy==.

#### Substance use: signs and treatment order?
- Signs: mood swings, irritability, ==declining grades==, withdrawal, bloodshot eyes, slurred speech.
- Order: ==assessment → detox → rehab/counseling → aftercare== (relapse prevention).

#### Suicide: what does the nurse do?
- Risks: depression, substance use, ==bullying==, family conflict, isolation.

> [!danger] Report immediately
> ==Ask directly== about self-harm and suicide; asking doesn't plant the idea `[ADDED]`. "I have thought about it" → ask about **plan and means**, don't leave alone, ==notify the provider==. Suicide risk outranks every other finding.

## VI. The hospitalized child (PEDS 5.1)

> [!quote] Anchor
> Hospitalization breaks routine and separates children from what they know. Stress slows recovery, so the nurse treats the family, not just the child.

### Family and communication

#### Family and sibling support?
- ==The family's response shapes the child's coping.== **Bowen family systems theory**: the family is one emotional unit; one member's stress affects all.
- Siblings feel resentment, jealousy, guilt → ==age-appropriate explanation==, video calls, ==steady home routine==.
- Parents: bring ==favorite items from home==; ==expect behavior changes==.

#### Stress-reducing interventions?
- ==Family-centered care== and family-centered rounds; consistent nurses; ==offer choices==; let the child hold safe equipment; **AIDET**.
- **Child life specialist** = therapeutic play. Play by age: infant music/rattles · toddler blocks, reading · preschool ==pretend play== · school-age board games, journaling · adolescent ==peers via devices==.

#### Discharge teaching: when and how verified?
- Start ==as early as possible==, confirm on discharge day. Verify with ==teach-back and return demonstration==, not "Any questions?"

### Admission and safety

#### Admission safety priorities?
- ==Latex allergy== (↑ risk: spina bifida, many surgeries; banana, avocado, kiwi `[ADDED]`); ==crib with protective top== for young children; non-slip socks; declutter.
- Professional ==medical interpreter==, not family `[ADDED for "not family"]`.

#### Infiltration vs. phlebitis? `[ADDED]`
| | Infiltration | Phlebitis |
|---|---|---|
| Signs | Swollen, ==cool==, pale | ==Warm==, red, cord along vein |
| Action | Stop, remove, elevate, restart elsewhere | Stop, remove, warm compress, restart elsewhere |

#### Restraint rules?
- ==Provider order==; ==remove ASAP==; monitor ROM, turning, skin, hydration, toileting. Check/release at least **q2h** `[ADDED — verify vs course materials]`; two fingers fit under; tie to bed frame.

> [!warning] Priority
> Restrained limb cool, pale, numb, or weak pulse → ==loosen and reassess circulation first== `[ADDED]`.

#### Who consents?
| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Minor < 18** | Parent/legal guardian; ==confirm custody first== |
| **Emancipated minor** | Signs own: ==married, military==, legally separated from parents |
| **Emergency** | ==Implied consent== |

- ==Provider explains== informed consent (diagnosis, procedure, risks, benefits, alternatives); nurse witnesses `[ADDED]`.

### Medications and measurements

#### Pediatric med safety?
- Dose by ==weight (mg/kg)==: lb **÷ 2.2** → kg, calculate safe range; ==outside range → hold and clarify== `[ADDED]`.
- ==Med reconciliation at admission, transfer, discharge.==
- Liquid meds **< 4 mo**: dropper/syringe ==slowly into the side of the mouth==; pacifier between. **4 mo–1 yr**: then **15 mL** diluted juice. Never mix into a full bottle `[ADDED]`.
- Infant IM: ==vastus lateralis==. Ear drops: pinna ==down and back < 3 yr==, up and back > 3 yr `[ADDED]`.

#### Output and diet orders?
- ==Weigh diapers==: wet − dry; **1 g = 1 mL** (wet **65 g** − dry **20 g** = **45 mL**). Adequate UO ≈ **1 mL/kg/hr** `[ADDED — verify vs course materials]`.
- Calories: infant **100 cal/kg/day**, **1–3 yr** **80**, **4–5 yr** **70**, **6–8 yr** **65**, **9+** **35–45 kcal/kg/day**. (10-kg infant = **1,000 cal/day**.)
- Clear liquids = see-through (broth, apple juice, gelatin); full = + ==milk, pudding==, cream soups `[ADDED]`.
- Healthy stoma = ==moist, pink-red==; pale, dusky, or black → report `[ADDED]`.

## VII. Communicable diseases (PEDS 5.2)

> [!quote] Anchor
> The nurse's main job is stopping spread: right isolation, right PPE, hand hygiene, and immunization.

### Infection control

#### Precaution types?
| | Contact | Droplet | Airborne |
|---|---|---|---|
| Room | Single preferred | Single preferred | ==Negative pressure== |
| PPE | ==Gown + gloves on entry== | Mask within **< 3 ft** | ==**N95** on before entering== |
| Transport | — | Child wears surgical mask | Child wears surgical mask `[ADDED]` |

#### PPE order?
- **On:** hand hygiene → gown → mask → goggles → ==gloves (over cuffs)==.
- **Off:** ==gloves (most contaminated)== → goggles (from back) → gown (inside out) → ==mask last (bottom ties, then top)== → hand hygiene.

### Disease master table

#### Which disease, which precaution, which priority?
| Disease | Hallmark | Isolation | Key nursing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Varicella** | Itchy vesicles → crusts, trunk/face first, all stages at once | ==Airborne + contact== | Contagious ==until all crusted==; ==no aspirin==; trim nails; ==no pregnant nurse== |
| **Measles (rubeola)** | Fever + cough, coryza, conjunctivitis → ==Koplik spots== → rash hairline down | ==Airborne== (in air **2 hr**) | Supportive; **vitamin A × 2 days**; cool-mist humidifier |
| **Rubella** `[ADDED]` | Mild pink rash, postauricular nodes | Droplet | ==Congenital rubella syndrome== → keep from pregnant people |
| **Mumps** | ==Parotitis== | Droplet `[ADDED]` | Contagious **1–2 days before → 5 days after**; soft diet, hot/cold compresses; orchitis |
| **Diphtheria** | ==Gray pseudomembrane== on throat | ==Contact + droplet== | ==Airway== + myocarditis watch; erythromycin/PCN G + antitoxin (DAT) |
| **Pertussis** `[ADDED]` | Paroxysmal cough, "whoop," ==infant apnea== | Droplet | DTaP/Tdap; cocooning |
| **Polio** | Flu-like → ==leg weakness/paralysis== | ==Contact== (fecal-oral) | Respiratory support; PT/OT; IPV |
| **COVID-19** | Often asymptomatic; ==MIS-C== weeks later | Per policy | Fever-free **24 hr** without meds; Paxlovid **≥ 12 yr + ≥ 40 kg** |
| **Mono (EBV)** | Fatigue, exudative tonsils, ==splenomegaly==; **15–24 yr** | Standard; no shared cups | ==No contact sports **4–6 wk**== |
| **Roseola (HHV-6)** | ==Fever **≥ 104 °F** × **3–5 days**, then rash== | Standard | Home until **24 hr** after fever; ==febrile seizure teaching== |
| **Fifth (parvo B19)** | ==Slapped cheek== → lacy rash | Standard | ==Not contagious once rash appears==; risk to pregnancy, sickle cell |
| **HFMD** | ==Painful mouth ulcers== + hand/foot rash; **< 5 yr** | Standard | Fluids; ==avoid hot drinks, acidic juice==; stool shedding ~**6 wk** |
| **Impetigo** | ==Honey-colored crusts== | Contact `[ADDED]` | Mupirocin (± cephalexin); bullous → PO antibiotic; ==APSGN teaching== |

#### Varicella fever: why not aspirin?
> [!danger] Report immediately
> **Reye syndrome** `[ADDED]`: encephalopathy + liver failure after viral illness treated with aspirin. Early sign ==persistent vomiting== → lethargy, confusion, seizures. Use ==acetaminophen==; check OTC labels for salicylates (bismuth subsalicylate).

#### Bacterial vs. viral meningitis?
| | Bacterial | Viral |
|---|---|---|
| Severity | ==Emergency==: sepsis, hearing loss, death | Usually self-limiting |
| CSF `[ADDED]` | Cloudy, ↑ neutrophils, ==↓ glucose==, ↑ protein | Clear, ↑ lymphocytes, normal glucose |
| Treatment | ==IV antibiotics==, corticosteroids; ==prophylaxis for close contacts==; droplet until **24 hr** of antibiotics `[ADDED]` | Antibiotics until bacterial ruled out; IV acyclovir |

- Signs: fever, headache, ==nuchal rigidity==, photophobia, Kernig/Brudzinski `[ADDED]`; infants: ==bulging fontanel==, high-pitched cry, poor feeding. Meningococcal → ==petechial rash==.
- ↑ ICP: irritability, vomiting, ↓ LOC; late ==Cushing triad== (bradycardia, widening pulse pressure, irregular breathing) `[ADDED]`.

#### Mono and roseola emergencies?
- Mono: ==sudden LUQ pain radiating to left shoulder==, dizziness, tachycardia = splenic rupture → report `[ADDED for signs]`.
- Febrile seizure: stay, side-lying, ==nothing in the mouth==, time it; ==call 911== (course).

#### Impetigo complication to teach?
- **APSGN** `[ADDED]`: ==tea/cola-colored urine==, puffy eyes, ↑ BP, ↓ UO ~**1–3 wk** after `[ADDED — verify]`. [NOTE: current practice: the deck links impetigo to rheumatic fever; rheumatic fever follows strep **pharyngitis**, and APSGN is the skin-infection complication.]

#### Hepatitis A vs. B vs. C?
| | Hep A | Hep B | Hep C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spread | ==Fecal-oral== | Blood/body fluids | Blood/body fluids |
| Findings | N/V, diarrhea | RUQ pain → ==jaundice, pale stool, dark urine== | Mostly ==asymptomatic== |
| Vaccine | Yes | Yes | ==No== |

#### Conjunctivitis: which type?
| | Bacterial | Viral | Allergic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drainage | ==Thick purulent, crusts lashes== | ==Watery== | ==None==; itchy, both eyes |
| Treatment | Antibiotic drops | Self-limiting | Antihistamine |

- Bacterial and viral are ==highly contagious==: no shared towels or eye makeup. Foreign body → flush, check for corneal abrasion.

#### Stomatitis: contagious or not?
- **Herpetic** (HSV): ==contagious==, high fever, swollen gums → ==acyclovir==. **Aphthous** (canker): ==not contagious==.
- Both → ==dehydration risk==; neutral-temperature, non-acidic foods.

#### Parasites: diagnosis and treatment?
| Parasite | Hallmark | Treatment / teaching |
|---|---|---|
| **Scabies** | ==Itching worse at night==, between fingers, burrows | **Permethrin**; ==treat close contacts==; hot-water laundry; itch may last **2–4 wk** `[ADDED — verify]` |
| **Lice** | ==Nits stuck to hair shaft== (dandruff flakes off) | **Permethrin**, repeat **1 wk**; nit comb; don't share hats |
| **Pinworm** | ==Perianal itching at night== | ==Tape test early morning==; **mebendazole** for ==whole household==, repeat **2 wk**; not with metronidazole |
| **Giardia** | Watery diarrhea, cramps, gas | ==**3** stool samples==; metronidazole; safe water; ==temporary dairy sensitivity== |

> [!warning] Priority
> **Lindane** and **ivermectin**: ==neurotoxicity== → avoid or use with caution in children (course). [NOTE: current practice: topical ivermectin **0.5%** is FDA-approved for head lice **≥ 6 mo**; lindane carries a boxed warning.]

## VIII. Immunity and immunizations (PEDS 5.2)

#### Types of immunity?
| Type | Example | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **Active** | Infection or ==vaccine== | Long-term (memory cells) |
| **Passive natural** | ==Placenta, breast milk== | Temporary |
| **Passive artificial** | ==Immune globulin== (HBIG, IVIG) | Immediate, temporary, no memory |
| **Herd** | Enough people immune | Protects the unvaccinated |

- **Antibodies:** IgG = long-term, crosses placenta · ==IgM = first in new infection== · IgA = secretions · IgE = allergy.
- **Toxoid** = inactivated toxin → active immunity (==diphtheria, tetanus==). **Antitoxin** = antibodies → ==immediate passive== immunity (DAT). **Attenuated** = live, weakened (==MMR, varicella, rotavirus==).
- **Cocooning** = vaccinate close contacts of vulnerable infants (e.g., Tdap for household) `[ADDED]`.

#### Contraindication vs. precaution?
- ==Only standard contraindication: severe allergic reaction to a prior dose or component.== Ask before every vaccine.
- ==Mild illness (ear infection, low-grade fever) → vaccinate.== Moderate/severe illness → delay.
- ==Live vaccines== (MMR, varicella, rotavirus, LAIV): ==not in pregnancy or immunocompromise== (e.g., SCID).

> [!tip] NCLEX tip
> 12-mo-old with an ear infection and low-grade fever due for MMR → ==give it==.

#### Routine schedule: what's tested?
| Vaccine | When | Watch |
|---|---|---|
| **Hep B** | ==Birth (within **24 hr**)==, **1–2 mo**, **6–18 mo** | — |
| **Rotavirus** (live, oral) | **2, 4** (± **6**) mo; ==start < **15 wk**, done by **8 mo**== | ==No if intussusception history or SCID== |
| **DTaP → Tdap** | **2, 4, 6, 15–18 mo, 4–6 yr**; Tdap **11–12**, then q**10 yr** | Precaution: Guillain-Barré |
| **Hib, PCV** | **2, 4, (6)** mo; booster **12–15 mo** | — |
| **IPV** | **2, 4, 6–18 mo, 4–6 yr** | — |
| **MMR, varicella** (live) | ==**12–15 mo**, **4–6 yr**== | No in pregnancy; ==neomycin== (both), ==gelatin== (varicella) allergy |
| **Hep A** | **12–23 mo**; 2nd **≥ 6 mo** later | — |
| **Influenza** | ==Yearly from **6 mo**== | LAIV live: no if immunosuppressed |
| **HPV** | Routine **11–12**, from **9**; catch-up to **26** (4.2) · **9–45** (5.2); 2 or 3 doses by start age | Severe yeast allergy |
| **MenACWY** | **11–12** + booster **16** | — |

- Intussusception (after rotavirus) `[ADDED]`: crampy pain, knees to chest, ==currant-jelly stools== → report.
- [NOTE: current practice: the January 2026 CDC schedule moved influenza, COVID-19, hep A, hep B, rotavirus, and meningococcal vaccines from universal to high-risk/shared decision-making; AAP still recommends them routinely. The course schedule is what's tested.]

#### Atraumatic vaccine administration? `[ADDED]`
- Infants: ==breastfeed or sucrose==, swaddle; all ages: topical anesthetic, ==comfort position on caregiver's lap==, distraction, ==most painful vaccine last==.
- Site: ==vastus lateralis== infants/toddlers; deltoid from ~**3 yr** (preferred **3–10 yr** `[3.2]`).
- Give the VIS; observe **15 min** (teen syncope); epinephrine ready.

#### Vaccine hesitancy and barriers?
- ==Actively correct myths== with evidence; empathetic risk/benefit talk. Interpreters, multilingual materials, evening/weekend clinics, free/low-cost programs.

## Self-quiz

> [!question]- 1. An 18-month-old has HR 145, RR 28, BP 90/44, axillary 37.7 °C. Which finding do you report?
> **HR 145**: above **80–140**. The rest are within range.

> [!question]- 2. Who do you see first: a 2-yr-old with inspiratory stridor, a 6-yr-old with a slapped-cheek rash, a 17-yr-old with mono asking about football, or a 9-yr-old with new bedwetting?
> The **2-yr-old with stridor**: upper airway obstruction (ABCs).

> [!question]- 3. A 4-yr-old says, "I got sick because I hit my brother." What concept is this, and what do you do?
> **Magical thinking** (preoperational). Ask what they think caused it and reassure them it isn't their fault.

> [!question]- 4. You're preparing a 4-yr-old for an IV. Which actions are correct?
> Demonstrate on a doll, let the child open the swab, keep the parent present, don't show the needle, say "poke," and do it in the treatment room.

> [!question]- 5. A 2-yr-old drinks 32 oz of cow's milk a day. What's the risk, and what do you teach?
> **Iron deficiency anemia.** Limit milk to **16–24 oz/day**; **≥ 3** servings of iron-rich foods.

> [!question]- 6. A toddler resists toilet training halfway through. What do you advise?
> Stop; the child isn't ready. Retry in **2–3 months**; never punish accidents.

> [!question]- 7. A 4-yr-old says, "I hungry." Expected?
> No. By **4**, sentences should have **> 4** words → language delay, speech pathologist referral.

> [!question]- 8. A 3-yr-old has bruises on the ears and abdomen, and the parent says "she falls a lot." Next action?
> Red flag (not over bony prominences). Document findings and direct quotes; report to **state social services**.

> [!question]- 9. A child says, "Grandpa told me a secret, and I'll get in trouble if I tell." Response?
> Explain that surprises are OK but secrets that hurt aren't; **never promise to keep it secret**; report as needed.

> [!question]- 10. How long is time-out for a 6-yr-old?
> **6 minutes** (1 min per year of age).

> [!question]- 11. An 11-yr-old has missed 14 school days, is withdrawn, and quit online games with friends. What do you assess?
> Interview privately for **bullying/cyberbullying**; psychosomatic symptoms; depression screening.

> [!question]- 12. Which firearm teaching is correct: "Show your child where the gun is kept" or "Store guns unloaded and locked, with ammunition stored separately"?
> **Unloaded, locked, ammo separate.** Safest is no gun in the home; ask about storage before visits.

> [!question]- 13. An 8-yr-old who was dry for a year starts wetting the bed. Type and action?
> **Secondary enuresis** → provider evaluation (UTI, neuro, stress).

> [!question]- 14. A child on desmopressin has headache, vomiting, and confusion. Concern?
> **Hyponatremia/water intoxication**: hold and notify.

> [!question]- 15. A parent reports "diarrhea" on a toilet-trained 6-yr-old's underwear and hard stools that clog the toilet. Problem and teaching?
> **Encopresis** from chronic constipation. High fiber, plenty of water, toileting routine, positive reinforcement.

> [!question]- 16. A 16-yr-old says, "I have thought about suicide." Priority?
> Ask about plan and means, don't leave alone, **notify the provider**.

> [!question]- 17. A teen on lisinopril wants to use a salt substitute. Response?
> Avoid it: salt substitutes contain potassium → **hyperkalemia**. Also avoid NSAIDs; report cough or facial swelling.

> [!question]- 18. A 16-yr-old's condom broke 2 days ago. Levonorgestrel teaching?
> Take now (within **72 hr**); not regular birth control; no STI protection; barrier/abstinence **7 days**; period late **> 7 days** → provider.

> [!question]- 19. A hospitalized toddler screamed for his mother all week; now he ignores her when she visits. Interpretation?
> **Detachment** (separation anxiety), not adjustment. Maximize parent presence and consistent caregivers.

> [!question]- 20. A 17-yr-old married client needs surgery. Who signs consent?
> **She does**: married minors are emancipated.

> [!question]- 21. Dry diaper 20 g, wet diaper 65 g. Output?
> **45 mL** (1 g = 1 mL).

> [!question]- 22. A child is admitted with measles. First action?
> **Airborne precautions**: negative-pressure room, N95 on before entering.

> [!question]- 23. An infant had 3 days of 104 °F fever and is now afebrile with a pink trunk rash. Diagnosis and teaching?
> **Roseola.** Contagious until **24 hr** after fever; teach febrile seizure response (side-lying, nothing in mouth, call 911).

> [!question]- 24. CSF is cloudy with low glucose and high neutrophils. Which meningitis, and what about close contacts?
> **Bacterial.** Close contacts get prophylactic antibiotics; droplet precautions.

> [!question]- 25. A 12-mo-old with an ear infection and low-grade fever is due for MMR. Action? And who can't get live vaccines?
> **Vaccinate** (mild illness). Live vaccines (MMR, varicella, rotavirus, LAIV) are withheld in **pregnancy** and **immunocompromise**.
